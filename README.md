## About

I'm a Software Engineering and Financial Engineering student at WorldQuant University (WQU). My current work centers on banking APIs, portfolio-allocation algorithms, and automating repeatable finance workflows. I am also developing deeper skills in data science, machine learning, full-stack development, and algorithmic trading.

My public repositories document the engineering foundations behind that direction: C, Python, Shell, Linux systems, APIs, command-line tools, and full-stack coursework. I am open to collaborating on data science, full-stack, fintech, machine-learning, and algorithmic-trading projects.

Away from code, I enjoy music, travel, movies, and video games—especially playing and building them.
